01 · The problem

Every existing approach is brittle in its own way.

AI-generated media is moving faster than the trust infrastructure around it. We surveyed the 2024–2025 state of the art before building anything.

Watermarks die under diffusion

TrustMark, SynthID, Stable Signature and VINE all decode to near-zero under guided-diffusion regeneration. Robust against compression — fragile against the next image model.

arxiv 2510.05978 · 2511.05598 · WAVES (NSF)

C2PA dies on every re-encode

Metadata is stripped by every social-media platform. Formal-methods analysis finds replaceable timestamps and no revocation checks. RAND: "end-to-end compliance is unrealistic in an open ecosystem."

arxiv 2604.24890 · NSA CSI 2025

AI detectors don't generalise

High in-distribution accuracy, poor cross-model transfer. Notable false-positive drops under simple perturbations. "No single method consistently outperforms others."

arxiv 2511.02791 · 2505.12335

No one measures the channel

Forensic literature on watermark bit-error patterns focuses on leak-attribution. Pixel-residual forensics need an original reference. Nothing uses the watermark itself as a forensic probe of an unknown channel.

The gap we picked.
02 · How it works

Register once. Verify anywhere.

Provena stamps an image with a cryptographically-signed manifest and an invisible watermark at registration, then runs a battery of independent probes at verification time.

STEP 01 / REGISTER

Sign & stamp

The image is hashed perceptually (pHash), the metadata is signed with Ed25519, and a 16-byte watermark payload is embedded into the luminance via hybrid DWT+DCT. The record lands in the append-only registry.

STEP 02 / SHARE

Survive the wild

The image travels through JPEG re-encodes, resizes, format conversions, social-media pipelines. Some signals survive cleanly. Some don't. The system reports what it sees — never what it wishes it saw.

STEP 03 / VERIFY

Read the receipt

On verification, every signal probes independently. The cryptographic signature is authoritative. The perceptual hash is tolerant. The watermark — and its bit-error pattern — measures the channel.

04 · The novel contribution

Echo Signature: turn the watermark inside out.

Every existing watermark system corrects bit errors and discards the error pattern as noise. Echo inverts the framing.

The probe is the watermark. The measurement is the error.

At registration, the DCT layer embeds a 48-bit payload at known coefficient positions in the green channel. At verification, we read both hard bits (0/1 thresholded decisions) and a soft signal (signed distance from the QIM decision boundary, normalized to [-1, +1]).

Comparing the extracted hard bits to the registered payload gives a bit-error pattern. From that pattern plus the soft signal we compute a six-component Echo Vector. Each component is quantized to 8 bits and encoded directly as 12 hex characters — not SHA-256-hashed. Plain hex preserves L1 distance in feature space, so similar processing histories produce Hamming-close fingerprints. Cross-image similarity is a real semantic metric, not a hash collision rate.

# fingerprint encoding — position-stable, L1-preserving fingerprint = ( ber 2x // hard-bit BER entropy 2x // spatial Shannon entropy low 2x // spectral low-pct high 2x // spectral high-pct soft_mean 2x // surviving signal strength soft_std 2x // channel uniformity ) // 12 hex chars · L1 distance preserved · no SHA-256
↳ Echo Vector · 6 components
  • bit_error_rate Hard-bit BER — channel damage magnitude
  • spatial_entropy Shannon entropy of error positions across a 4×4 image grid (bits)
  • spectral_low_pct Fraction of error energy in low DCT frequencies — blur signature
  • spectral_high_pct Fraction in high frequencies — compression / sharpen signature
  • soft_mean_abs Mean |soft signal| — strength of surviving signal
  • soft_std Std of soft signal — mixed vs uniform channel

Validated experimentally

10/10 unique fingerprints across a 10-channel sweep. Same-channel pairs cluster across different content. Different-channel pairs separate. Reproducible.

05 · Honest limits

What Provena does not claim.

Marketing tone is for vendors. Real systems disclose where they break.

Not C2PA-compliant

Provena emits C2PA-shaped JSON with an Ed25519 signature, not output from the official c2pa-python SDK. Real C2PA verifiers will not accept these manifests.

Not an AI-image detector

Classifier-based detectors don't generalise across models. Provena measures provenance and channel, not "is this image AI."

Not federated

The registry is local. Cross-attestation (the Provenance Mesh) is sketched but not built. There is no single source of truth across operators.

Not a verdict engine

The heuristic forensic analyzer false-positives on every natural image and is disabled. Each signal reports independently; the human reads the receipt.

DCT layer is fragile by design

Coefficient (4,3) saturates between JPEG q≈75 and q≈30 — and that's the point. The fragility is what makes the layer a useful channel probe for Echo Signature.

Echo needs ground truth

The Echo primitive requires the canonical registered payload. On unregistered images, edit_trace correctly reports absent.

06 · What's next

Open threads we're chasing.

The Photo Receipt and Echo Signature are shipping today. Three meaningful gaps remain.

Federated cross-attestation via daily Merkle roots. Closes the C2PA single-source-of-truth gap. Needs a multi-node demo before it ships.

Swap our custom JSON-with-signature for c2pa-python manifests so the system interoperates with the broader content-credentials ecosystem.
