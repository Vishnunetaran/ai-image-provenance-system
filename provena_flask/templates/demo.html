01 · The problem

Five attack surfaces. Our system defends all of them.

AI-generated media is moving faster than the trust infrastructure around it. Most provenance systems fail against even basic attacks.

Watermarks die under compression

TrustMark, SynthID, Stable Signature all decode to near-zero under diffusion regeneration. Robust against mild JPEG — fragile against the next image model.

arxiv 2510.05978 · 2511.05598 · WAVES (NSF)

Metadata strips on every platform

C2PA manifests are immediately stripped by Twitter, WhatsApp, Instagram, and every social media upload pipeline. One re-share destroys the entire metadata chain.

arxiv 2604.24890 · NSA CSI 2025

Cropped fragments lose provenance

A 200×200px screenshot crop destroys all classic watermarks. No existing system can recover provenance from a fragment without the original image as reference.

The gap SIFT homography solves.

Splicing attacks bypass registry

Attackers transfer watermarks from a registered image to a different tampered image to fake provenance. Standard systems cannot detect this swap.

Solved by SIFT ID ↔ watermark payload cross-check.
02 · How it works

Register once. Verify anything.

PROVENA stamps an image with cryptographic proof and five invisible watermarks at registration, then runs a full forensic battery at verification — even on cropped screenshots.

STEP 01 / REGISTER

Sign, stamp & index

The image is hashed perceptually (pHash), the metadata is signed with Ed25519 into a C2PA-shaped manifest, and 1,000 SIFT keypoints are indexed for fragment retrieval. Then the HydraWatermark V4 runs five layers sequentially: Neural TrustMark, DCT Block QIM, Tiled Holographic DCT, Spatial LSB+ECC, and Tamper Grid CRC-8. The record lands in the append-only SQLite registry.

STEP 02 / SURVIVE

Survive the wild

The watermarked image travels through JPEG re-encodes, social media pipelines, format conversions, screenshots, and crops. Some layers survive cleanly. Some degrade. SIFT keypoints survive everything short of full pixel destruction — they are geometry, not pixels. The system reports exactly what it sees.

STEP 03 / VERIFY

Read the forensic receipt

On verification, every signal probes independently. SIFT extracts keypoints from the query, RANSAC finds homography matrix H, and the fragment is warped back to original coordinates. All five watermark layers then extract and vote. Splicing is detected by cross-checking the SIFT record ID against the watermark payload ID — a mismatch is proof of tampering.

03 · The math, in plain English

What's actually inside the receipt?

Two core innovations do most of the work. One is an old technique made holographic; one is a geometric primitive nobody applies to watermark recovery this way.

HydraWatermark V4 · 5 layers

Five independent watermarks. An attacker has to defeat all five simultaneously.

Like a painting with five hidden signatures in different media — ink, UV, magnetic, microprint, and chemical. Each lives in a different mathematical domain. To erase provenance cleanly, every one must be found and removed at the same time.

Layer 1 · Neural (TrustMark). A learned encoder/decoder network hiding 48 bits of payload in image features. Trained to survive JPEG compression, crops, and social-media re-encodes.

Layer 2 · DCT Block QIM. Modulates 8×8 DCT coefficients in the frequency domain. Resilient to spatial attacks like blur and mild rotation.

Layer 3 · Tiled Holographic DCT. Slides a 128×128 window at 50% overlap — each tile independently encodes the full 48-bit payload. Barker-13 sync codes detect tile boundaries after severe crops.

Layer 4 · Spatial LSB + ECC. Pixel-level least-significant-bit modulation seeded by image dimensions, protected by Reed-Solomon error correction. The fallback signal of last resort.

Layer 5 · Tamper Grid CRC-8. Divides the image into an adaptive grid; each cell encodes position + record ID + CRC-8 checksum. Returns a pixel-perfect tamper_map showing which regions are authentic and which are modified.

SIFT Homography · Geometric Alignment · novel

Fragment matching without the watermark: SIFT keypoints survive everything.

Like a detective matching a cropped witness photo to a full CCTV frame using distinctive features — a scar, a tattoo, a jacket pattern. No matter how the crop is framed, the geometric features still align.

Keypoint extraction. SIFT extracts up to 1,000 stable keypoints + 128-dimensional descriptors per image. Capped by nfeatures=1000 to prevent DoS flooding.

Lowe's Ratio Test (0.75). Filters ambiguous, repetitive, and near-collinear descriptor matches before RANSAC runs.

RANSAC Homography. Finds 4-DOF perspective transform matrix H with ≥12 geometrically consistent inliers at 5px error tolerance. H is validated: positive determinant (10⁻⁴ to 10⁴), convex projected polygon.

Splicing detection. The record ID extracted from the watermark payload is cross-checked against the record matched by SIFT. A mismatch is definitive proof of a watermark transfer attack → verdict TAMPERED.

04 · The Forensic Receipt

Seven signals. None of them lie.

Every signal reports independently. Humans interpret the receipt — the system never invents a verdict.

01
c2pa_manifest

Is there an Ed25519-signed C2PA manifest in the file's EXIF, and does its signature verify against this image's perceptual hash?

02
neural_trustmark

Did the TrustMark neural decoder extract a valid 48-bit payload that resolves to a registry record? Survives JPEG and mild crops.

03
dct_block_qim

Did the frequency-domain QIM extractor recover the payload from 8×8 DCT blocks? Resilient to spatial attacks like blur and noise.

04
tiled_holographic

Holographic 128×128 sliding-window extraction with Barker-13 sync detection. Recovers payload from severely cropped fragments that break all other layers.

05
tamper_grid

Per-cell CRC-8 verification across an adaptive grid. Returns a pixel-perfect map of which regions are authentic, tampered, or outside the aligned crop.

06
sift_homography

SIFT keypoint + RANSAC homography geometric alignment. Finds the parent image from a cropped screenshot, warps it back, and cross-checks for splicing attacks.

07
phash_registry

64-bit perceptual hash Hamming-distance fallback. Matches near-duplicate images even when all watermarks and metadata are destroyed.

05 · Honest limits

What PROVENA does not claim.

Marketing tone is for vendors. Real systems disclose exactly where they break.

Not regeneration-proof

Guided-diffusion image regeneration destroys all current watermarks including ours. This is an open research problem. No system defeats it today.

SIFT requires texture

Featureless or uniform images — solid gradients, blank backgrounds — produce fewer than 12 keypoints. Geometric fragment matching cannot be performed on flat images.

Timestamps are self-reported

We cryptographically bind timestamps into the Ed25519 signature, but we do not independently verify when an image was generated. A caller can lie about the timestamp.

Not federated

The registry is local. Cross-operator attestation — a Provenance Mesh — is architecturally planned but not yet built. There is no single source of truth across operators.
