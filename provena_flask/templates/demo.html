CRYPTOGRAPHIC PROVENANCE · RESEARCH BUILD

Proof of origin for AI images.

Provena establishes verifiable provenance for AI-generated images using Ed25519 digital signatures and an append-only registry, supplemented by perceptual hashing and a hybrid DWT+DCT watermark.

Try Now → How it works
01 / SIGNATURE

Ed25519 cryptography

Industry-standard elliptic-curve signatures bind metadata to every image. Tampering is detected instantly.

02 / REGISTRY

Append-only ledger

Provenance records live in a tamper-evident SQLite ledger with a complete audit trail.

03 / pHASH

Perceptual matching

Hamming-distance pHash matching survives compression, resizing, and minor edits.

04 / WATERMARK

Hybrid DWT+DCT

Imperceptible forensic trace embedded in the luminance channel, with redundancy and a sync prefix.
